The F32 scaled magnitude operation/order is adapted from NumPy v1.23.5,
numpy/core/src/umath/simd.inc.src, avx512_cabsolute_ps.
The bounded F64 cosine operation/order and coefficients are adapted from
NumPy/SVML commit 1c5260a61e7dce6be48073dfa96291edb0a11d79,
linux/avx512/svml_z0_cos_d_la.s, __svml_cos8 fast path.
The bounded F64 sine operation/order and coefficients are adapted from the
same revision, linux/avx512/svml_z0_sin_d_la.s, __svml_sin8 fast path.
